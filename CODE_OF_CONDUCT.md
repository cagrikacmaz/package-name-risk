# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Project participants are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome constructive feedback, focus criticism on the work, and avoid harassment or discriminatory behavior. Examples of unacceptable behavior include personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
